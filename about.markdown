Welcome to <em>(inaudible)</em>, other selves! On this podcast we dig into a spiritual philosophy popularly known as [the Law of One](http://lawofone.info) and the telepathically received messages that help articulate it. Hosts Jeremy Weiland and Jamie Liestman provide commentary on and analysis of this philosophy, often directly addressing a transcript of a channeling session to help the listener grasp nuances that stretch across decades of seeking in order to apply these ideas directly to our everyday human lives. After all, there's a lot of fantastic information in these messages that cannot be empirically proven by the layman, so Jeremy and Jamie focus on information one can prove within the material of one's own life, validating for one's own self what is true about this metaphysical philosophy.

Your current hosts on this podcast are Jamie Liestman and Jeremy Weiland. Jamie is an [Other Selves Working Group](https://otherselvesworking.group) member and longtime seeker in the Confederation tradition specializing in dreams. Jeremy has studied Confederation philosophy for over a quarter century and is the co-founder of the Working Group as well as the [High Altitude Receiving Center](https://har.center). Past hosts include Nithin Reddy and Ryan Masterson, the latter of whom started the [Living Love and Light](https://anchor.fm/ryan-masterson) podcast that inspired this project. 

Bringing their highest and best intentions to parsing these messages, these hosts seek to find in these weird, beautiful, inspiring words that inaudible love that this Confederation claims pervades all things.
