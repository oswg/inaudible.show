In this long overdue episode, Jamie and Jeremy discuss one of the aspects of living in illusion that always catches us with our fly down: embarrassment. Jamie brings up the idea that embarrassment is just an indication of moving past one's conditioning, and Jeremy frames this movement as a way to talk about the fragility of the personality that we drag through catalyst. Shame and embarrassment seem to serve as load bearing pylons for the entire veiled third density experience. As we begin to sit with these experiences and build a more honest relationship with our many selves within and without, we learn how to use shame and embarrassment to identify what needs balance and forgiveness.
