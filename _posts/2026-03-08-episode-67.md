Jamie and Jeremy turn to the Confederation's thoughts on mated relationships in this episode, drawing upon their personal lives while they attempting to digest and ground their novel insights in the everyday experience of these intimate, complex, and consequential partnerships. The Confederation tends to stress the importance of knowing oneself honestly since this helps the seeker becoming more open and vulnerable with the mate so as to make relationship catalyst more potent and growth more urgent. Through meditation we tap into the sacred nature of ourself, the other self, and the resulting relationship-self, and this provides the basis for living and evolving at every level of awareness through the faithful mirroring of the mate.
