<p>Thanks for visiting our site. <em>Inaudible</em> is a new podcast focused on discussing the messages of the Confederation of Planets in the Service of the Infinite Creator as channeled by members of L/L Research. These messages can be listened to on our sister podcast, Living Love and Light, which is run by Ryan Masterson. Jeremy Weiland joins Ryan on <em>inaudible</em> to digest the themes of these messages and place them in a broader context of Confederation philosophy. We hope these recorded conversations can be a useful resource for seekers on their own path.</p>

<p>We released our first episode this week, and the current plan is to release weekly. If you have any feedback, questions, suggested topics, or anything else you'd like to share, feel free to contact us.</p>
