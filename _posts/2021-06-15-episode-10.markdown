Happy Tuesday, other selves! In this episode, Ryan and I discuss the final two centers and their mystical, mysterious nature. We then turn to <a href="https://llresearch.org/transcripts/issues/2000/2000_0507.aspx">a Q'uo transcript</a> that discusses emotions and their purpose on the evolutionary path. We only get about halfway through reading it, but there is a tie-in between indigo ray and what those of Q'uo call the "way of wisdom" that attempts to transcend emotion. This more intellectual approach to work in consciousness is distinguished from the "way of the open heart" that faces emotions head-on. Next week, we'll finish analyzing the transcript and focus on the purification of these emotions.
